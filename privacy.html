Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

V&DG Management LLC (“V&DG,” “we,” “us”) is a Florida limited liability company. This policy explains what information we collect through vdgmanagement.com (the “Site”), how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, we receive the details you type in: your name, work email, organization, role and your message.
  • Email. If you email us at hello@vdgmanagement.com, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Technical data. The Site is hosted by a third-party web hosting provider. Like most web hosts, the host and our form service may process technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as part of delivering the Site and the form.

The Site does not use advertising trackers and does not ask you to create an account. We do not knowingly use cookies on the Site. If we add analytics or cookies in the future, we will update this policy before we do.

Please do not include patient information or other sensitive personal information in the form or in an email.

How we use information

We use what you send us to read and reply to your message, to scope and deliver advisory services you ask about, to keep business records, and to protect the Site and meet legal obligations.

Who handles it

Contact-form messages are passed through a third-party form-processing service, which forwards them to our email. The Site is delivered by a third-party web hosting provider. Our email is provided by a business email service. These providers process information for us to run the Site and our email. We share information with others only when you ask us to or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails and form messages for as long as needed to respond and to keep ordinary business records, and then delete them or keep them only as the law requires. If we work together, related records are kept for the period our business and legal obligations call for.

Your rights and choices

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it or delete it. Write to hello@vdgmanagement.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, and within any period the law sets.

US state privacy rights. Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to appeal a decision about a request. To make a request, email the address above. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

Children

The Site is meant for business visitors and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we receive. No method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites, such as LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
