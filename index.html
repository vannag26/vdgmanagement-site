Questions

Before you book

What do I get from the session?

A 90-minute working session with an executive advisor on what AI is already running, who owns it, and whether the next spend can be defended. You walk out with a 90-day order of work, the first owner to name, and the first tool that needs an off switch. There is no obligation to buy anything after it.

Why not just a free call?

A free call is a sales call. This is the work itself, and the plan is yours to keep whether or not we work together again. Scope and fee are confirmed by email before anything is scheduled, and if your question isn’t a fit, we say so then.

Do you handle patient data or sign a BAA?

No. V&DG is administrative, operational and governance work only. We do not access patient data or EHRs, we do not sign a BAA, and we ask that you never send PHI.

How quickly can we start?

As soon as we confirm fit by email. There is no software to install, no integration and no data access to arrange, so nothing has to go through IT before the session.

What happens after the session?

You leave with the 90-day plan and run it however you choose. If more help makes sense, the next steps are a readiness diagnostic, a governance assessment, or ongoing fractional advisory. Each is scoped and priced separately, and none is required.

I run a small business. Which option is for me?

Start with the $150 AI Operations Audit. It’s a written review of 1 to 3 workflows with ranked opportunities, quick wins and a risk checklist, ordered through Fiverr. The Executive AI Strategy Session is designed for health-system and enterprise leaders.

Is this software?

No. We do not sell software, and we do not ask you to replace the tools you already have. V&DG is a service: advice and hands-on help to make the AI you already have owned, governed and measurable.
